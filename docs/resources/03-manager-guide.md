# Manager Guide — Performance & Growth Cycle

Your end-to-end guide to the quarterly cycle: how to assess each direct report, how to share and run the 1:1, how to close out, and how the Review and Planning rhythm with People supports you.

## Your dashboard

The manager dashboard shows:

- **Your own cycle** at the top: you complete a self-assessment and get reviewed like everyone else.
- **Self-assessments done / manager assessments done** counts for your team.
- The **meeting pipeline**: a board with one card per direct report, moving through **In Assessment → Ready to Meet → Meeting Scheduled → Meeting Complete → Review Complete**. Each column shows its due date; the timeline above shows the three milestones for the cycle. Cards show a tracking dot (on track, due soon, overdue) and the actions available at that stage.
- **Team tasks**: open actions across your reports.

A card moves from In Assessment to Ready to Meet automatically once both assessments are submitted. You drag it to Meeting Scheduled when the 1:1 is booked; completing the meeting moves it to Meeting Complete; sending results moves it to Review Complete. You can also filter the board by job title and tenure.

## Before the cycle: goals and key metrics

Every report needs current **key metrics** (recurring measures for the role) and **goals** (time-bound commitments) before the cycle opens. Open **My Team → View Profile** to add or edit them, or ask the person to set their own from Goals & Metrics. Items attached to a job title by an admin appear automatically as **Role-set** and apply to everyone in that role. See *Setting up Goals and Key Metrics* for detail and examples.

If a profile is empty when the cycle opens, the assessment will feel ungrounded and you'll lose the value of the framework for that quarter.

## Completing the assessment

Click **Assess** on a report's card (or **Assess** / **Edit Review** on My Team). The form has the same five sections as the self-assessment, and the same **One at a time / All on one page** toggle.

**Rate first, then read.** You rate each section without seeing what the person wrote. The app enforces this: each section has a *"Show what {name} said"* panel that only unlocks once your ratings for that section are saved. That's deliberate. The gap between your view and theirs is the point of the conversation, so don't pre-adjust to avoid a hard one.

**1. Performance.** Below the rating is the **Goals & Key Metrics** panel. Record or confirm the actual result against each key metric, mark it **Hit / Missed / Unsure**, and record your own progress update on each goal (the person's update shows alongside yours). You can't move past this section until every key metric has a result.

Then rate performance (1–3) with evidence, describe the achievements you observed, and the blockers you'll help remove.

**Rating versus results.** If you rate performance as Meeting or above while more metrics were missed than hit, a warning appears on the section and on the summary. Either your evidence carries the rating, or the targets need a conversation. Leadership can see these disconnects on Analysis.

**2. Growth Readiness.** How ready are they to take on more? Developing (building the basics), Building (growing into more), Ready Now (could step up today). Give the evidence: stretch work, learning agility, feedback sought and applied.

**3. Values Alignment.** Rate each of the four values separately, with prompts to consider, and give examples of values-aligned behaviour. The overall values score is the rounded average.

**4. Engagement.** Rate how engaged they appear, what you observe driving it, and the support you'll commit to next quarter.

**5. Looking Ahead.** Priorities and notes for their next quarter.

**Reveal and respond.** Once a section's ratings are saved, click *Show what {name} said*. Their rating and comments for that section appear; write your response beneath. Respond to what they actually wrote: acknowledge it, add context, or say where you see it differently. If they haven't submitted yet, you can submit now and come back to respond later; responses stay editable right through, including after results are sent.

**Submit.** Submit needs every rating set. After submitting you can still **Edit assessment** until results are sent; every change is logged in the audit trail on the summary page.

## Feedback principles

**Be specific.** Every rating needs at least one concrete example you're prepared to share. "They're doing fine" is not evidence.

**Rate the work, not the person.** You're assessing output and behaviours, not character.

**Don't anchor to the self-assessment.** The app won't let you see it before you rate. Keep that spirit when you edit afterwards: adjust for new evidence, not to soften the conversation.

**Let the metrics anchor performance.** You've just marked each one hit or missed. If the rating and the record disagree, say why in the evidence.

## Share the review before the 1:1

Once you've submitted, **share** the review: *Share review* on the Kanban card, on the summary page, or on the meeting page. Sharing makes your ratings, comments and responses visible to the person on their dashboard and My Reviews, and emails them with a calendar link for the 1:1. The aim is that they walk into the meeting having read it, so the time goes on the conversation rather than the reveal.

You can share before their self-assessment is in (the app warns you); you'll just have no comments to respond to yet.

**Editing after sharing.** You can still change ratings and comments, but the form asks for a one-line reason. The reason is recorded in the audit trail, and the person sees an *Updated since shared* badge and gets an email. Responses don't need a reason.

## Running the 1:1

Book it with *Add to Google Calendar* or the *.ics* link on the meeting page, drag the card to **Meeting Scheduled**, then open the meeting from the card (**Start Meeting**) or from the summary.

The meeting page gives you everything in one place: the two 9-box placements with prescribed actions, what you both said section by section (already expanded, including your responses), goals and key-metric results, then notes and actions. It prints cleanly if you want it on paper.

The 1:1 is 45 minutes, structured, everything in the open.

- **0–5 min: check-in.** How are they going? Anything outside work affecting them? Start human.
- **5–15 min: their self-assessment.** They walk through it. You listen and ask clarifying questions. Don't correct or react yet.
- **15–25 min: your feedback on Performance and Growth Readiness.** Lead with the rating, then the rationale. They've read it; go straight to where you differ and why.
- **25–30 min: Values and Engagement.** The per-value breakdown and the engagement rating, with rationale.
- **30–40 min: the gap.** The most important part. Where do your views diverge? What are they not seeing? What are you not seeing? Stay curious.
- **40–45 min: actions and forward look.** Confirm the prescribed actions, agree priorities and goal changes. Write them into the meeting as actions.

**Notes and actions.** Type notes as you go and **Save notes**. Add each agreed action with an owner and due date; each becomes a task visible to both of you on the Tasks page. Nothing agreed verbally and forgotten.

**Complete the meeting.** Click **Complete Meeting**. The card moves to Meeting Complete, the person is emailed, and your notes appear on their My Reviews.

### In the room

**Lead with the rating before you explain it.** Don't build up to the number.

**Separate performance from person.** "Your output on the BAS lodgements was below what we need" is feedback. "You're not good enough" is not.

**Invite disagreement.** Ask "does this feel fair?" and listen. If they push back with evidence, be willing to adjust, with a reason, after the meeting.

**Close the loop.** Every 1:1 ends with written actions both of you can see.

## Close out: send results

From the Meeting Complete column (or the summary page) click **Send Results**. This closes the cycle for that person: the card moves to Review Complete, their dashboard shows *Review Complete*, and the assessment is locked. If you need to change a rating afterwards, **Unlock & edit** on the assess page re-opens it (logged) and you can re-send.

The summary page asks you to confirm you've held the 1:1 if the meeting isn't marked complete yet.

## How the ratings work together

Your four ratings produce two placements, each with a prescribed action you commit to.

**Talent Density: Performance × Growth Readiness.** Capability and development.

| | Perf Below (1) | Perf Meeting (2) | Perf Exceeding (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ready Now (3)** | **Remove the Blockers.** Diagnose the blocker. 30-day action plan. Review fortnightly. | **Stretch & Develop.** Assign a stretch goal or project. Set a 90-day development goal. | **Ready to Promote.** Career conversation. Discuss promotion or expanded scope this quarter. |
| **Building (2)** | **Build the Foundations.** 30-day improvement plan with measurable outcomes. | **Keep Growing.** Acknowledge reliability. Set one small growth goal. | **Expand the Role.** Assign stretch goals if aligned to their ambitions. |
| **Developing (1)** | **Intervene Now.** Direct conversation this week. Move to PIP. | **Maintain & Recognise.** Clarify expectations. Ask if they want more or are content. | **Deepen the Expertise.** Recognise expertise. Protect from burnout. |

**Cultural Momentum: Values Alignment × Engagement.** Culture and motivation.

| | Values Below (1) | Values Meeting (2) | Values Exceeding (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engaged Exceeding (3)** | **Redirect the Energy.** 30-day focus on 1–2 specific values with examples. | **Reinforce the Behaviours.** Call out the values behaviours you want more of. | **Protect & Amplify.** Look for further cultural impact opportunities. |
| **Engaged Meeting (2)** | **Have the Conversation.** Direct conversation on alignment. No shift in two months → escalate. | **Stay the Course.** One intentional check-in question per 1:1. | **Invest Deeper.** Ask what would deepen their engagement. |
| **Engaged Below (1)** | **Assess the Fit.** Honest assessment on fit. | **Find What's Draining.** Intervene now. Offer tangible relief ASAP. | **Re-energise Now.** Identify what's draining them. Create actions. |

Both placements and their actions are on the summary page, the meeting page, and the person's My Reviews.

## Analysis

**Analysis** in the sidebar shows your team on both grids for any cycle (or averaged across a range of quarters). Use it when preparing conversations and before your Review and Planning meeting.

- Switch between **Talent Density** and **Cultural Momentum**; view **Names**, **Counts** or a **Heatmap**; every cell shows its count and share.
- The **tier bar** rolls the nine boxes into Top, Medium and Low talent.
- Filter by title, team, manager and tenure.
- **Key insights** show averages with the trend against last quarter.
- **Quarter-over-quarter movement** lists each person's ratings, tier and who they report to, with arrows for change.
- **Rating vs results** lists anyone rated Meeting or above whose metrics were mostly missed.
- **By manager** shows, for each manager, reviews submitted against reports, average scores, share in the top tier, reviews edited after submission, ratings adjusted after reading the self-assessment, and disconnects. Leadership uses this table; it's the accountability view.
- **Prescribed actions** lists the action for every person on the selected grid.

## Your Review and Planning rhythm with People

Alongside the quarterly cycle, you have a regular **Review and Planning** meeting with the People Lead. This is where organisation-level consistency lives, and where you get real-time support.

**Purpose.** How your team is tracking, the ratings you've given, development actions, performance and resourcing, early risks, consistency across managers, and patterns you want help thinking through.

**What to bring.** Anyone you've rated high-concern or high-potential, recent 1:1 actions, themes across the team, and the Analysis views for your team. Between meetings, the door is open: if something material comes up, reach out rather than wait.

## Manager quick reference

After each cycle, answer five questions for every direct report:

1. What's their Talent Density action, and what's the one thing I'll do?
2. What's their Cultural Momentum action, and what's the one thing I'll do?
3. Do the two actions conflict? If so, which takes priority?
4. What's the trend against last quarter: improving, stable or declining?
5. What am I committing to do differently this quarter, and is it written down as an action?

## Checklist for the cycle

- [ ] Goals and key metrics current for every report before the cycle opens
- [ ] Results recorded (hit / missed) for every key metric
- [ ] Every section rated, revealed and responded to; submitted by the Ready to Meet date
- [ ] Review shared with the person before the 1:1
- [ ] 1:1 booked (calendar link), card moved to Meeting Scheduled
- [ ] Meeting run from the meeting page; notes saved; actions added with owners and dates; Complete Meeting clicked by the Meeting Complete date
- [ ] Results sent by the Results Sent date
- [ ] Analysis checked: anything in Rating vs results or the By manager row to act on?
