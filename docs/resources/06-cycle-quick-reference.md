# Cycle Quick Reference

One page: who does what, when, and what each status means.

## The sequence

| Step | Team member | Manager | Status on the board |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cycle opens | Check goals and key metrics are current | Check every report's goals and metrics | In Assessment |
| Record results | Enter actual results, mark hit / missed, update goal progress | Confirm results, add your goal updates | In Assessment |
| Assess | Complete the five sections, submit | Rate each section blind, reveal, respond, submit | In Assessment → **Ready to Meet** |
| Share | Read the review on My Reviews before the 1:1 | **Share review** | Ready to Meet |
| Book | | Calendar link; drag card to Meeting Scheduled | **Meeting Scheduled** |
| 1:1 | Walk through your self-assessment, discuss the gaps, agree actions | Run from the meeting page; notes and actions; **Complete Meeting** | **Meeting Complete** |
| Close out | Read notes and actions on My Reviews and Tasks | **Send Results** | **Review Complete** |
| Through the quarter | Work the actions; keep goals current | Work the actions; Review and Planning with People | |

## The dates

Each cycle has three milestone dates, set when it's opened and shown on the dashboard timeline:

- **Ready to Meet**: both assessments submitted.
- **Meeting Complete**: 1:1 held and marked complete.
- **Results Sent**: results sent, cycle closed.

Reminder emails go out Sunday and Monday mornings for anything outstanding; a manager's manager is copied three days past a milestone.

## The five sections

Performance · Growth Readiness · Values Alignment (four values) · Engagement · Looking Ahead. Each rating is 1–3. Submit needs every rating set.

## Rating labels

| | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance, Values, Engagement | Below | Meeting | Exceeding |
| Growth Readiness | Developing | Building | Ready Now |

## Placements and tiers

**Talent Density** (Performance × Growth) and **Cultural Momentum** (Values × Engagement), nine boxes each, every box with a prescribed action. Tiers: **Top** = Ready to Promote, Stretch & Develop, Expand the Role · **Medium** = Keep Growing, Deepen the Expertise, Remove the Blockers · **Low** = Intervene Now, Build the Foundations, Maintain & Recognise.

## Badges you'll see

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Self-assessment is in |
| **Not yet shared** / **Shared** | Whether the manager's review is visible to the team member yet |
| **Shared ahead of 1:1** | Review shared; read it before the meeting |
| **Updated since shared** | A rating or comment changed after sharing; re-read |
| **Review Complete** | Results sent; cycle closed for this person |
| **Role-set** | Goal or metric attached to the job title; managed by admin |
| **Set by employee** / **Set by manager** | Who created the goal or metric |
| **Hit** / **Missed** | Metric result against target for the cycle |
