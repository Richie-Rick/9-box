# Performance & Growth Cycle — Process Overview

The most valuable part of this isn't the ratings. It's the gap between how you see yourself and how your manager sees you, and the conversation that gap opens up. Everything in the process is built to make that conversation honest, prepared and useful.

## The principles

**Transparency.** Every rating is shared, with the rationale behind it. Your manager shares their review with you *before* your 1:1 so you walk in having read it. Nothing is hidden and nothing is sprung on you in the room.

**Growth orientation.** This exists to develop you, not just evaluate you. Every cycle ends with agreed actions, and each 9-box placement comes with a prescribed action your manager commits to.

**Consistency.** One quarterly cadence and one rating model across every team. The same five sections, the same three-point scales, the same milestones.

**Accountability.** Both of you own it. You complete a self-assessment; your manager completes theirs *without* seeing yours; then they read yours and respond to it in writing.

**Grounded in evidence.** Goals and key metrics are reviewed and results recorded before anyone rates anything, so the conversation starts from what actually happened, not from how the quarter felt.

**Simplicity.** Three-point scales across the board. Clear and decisive; no hiding in the middle.

## The quarterly rhythm

Cycles run quarterly (Q1 January–March, Q2 April–June, Q3 July–September, Q4 October–December). The review happens in the month after the quarter closes. Each cycle has three milestone dates, set by the admin when the cycle is opened and shown on your dashboard:

| Milestone | What it means | Typical date |
|---|---|---|
| **Ready to Meet** | Your self-assessment and your manager's assessment are both submitted | 10th of the month after the quarter |
| **Meeting Complete** | Your 1:1 has been held and marked complete | 20th |
| **Results Sent** | Results are sent and the cycle is closed for you | 25th |

Q2 ends on the financial-year close, so its review lands a month later than the others. Dates can differ cycle to cycle; the ones that apply are always on the dashboard timeline.

Who completes a cycle: anyone who worked six weeks or more of the quarter. People who started later set up their goals and metrics and join the next cycle.

## The five sections

Both the self-assessment and the manager assessment are built from the same five sections. Where you answer a question about yourself, your manager answers its mirror about you. The two are compared side by side afterwards.

| Section | You rate / describe | Your manager rates / describes |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance** | Your performance (1–3), the evidence, key achievements, blockers, what you learned | Your performance (1–3), the evidence, achievements they observed, blockers they'll help remove |
| **Growth Readiness** | How ready you are to take on more (1–3), where you want to grow | How ready you are to take on more (1–3), the evidence |
| **Values Alignment** | Each of the four values (1–3), examples | Each of the four values (1–3), examples |
| **Engagement** | How engaged you feel (1–3), what's driving it, the support you need | How engaged you appear (1–3), what they observe, the support they'll commit to |
| **Looking Ahead** | Your goals for next quarter (tracked goals, not free text) | Priorities and notes for your next quarter |

Your manager also writes a **response** to your comments in each of the first four sections, after reading them.

## The rating scales

**Performance** measures delivery against your role requirements, grounded in your key metrics and goals.

- **3 — Exceeding:** Frequently surpassing what the role requires. Takes initiative, delivers above the bar, measurably lifts outcomes.
- **2 — Meeting:** Reliably delivering on all core role requirements. Consistent, on time, meets the expected standard.
- **1 — Below:** Consistently missing key metrics or role requirements. Quality, timeliness or reliability issues are evident and recurring.

**Growth Readiness** measures how ready you are to expand beyond your current role.

- **3 — Ready Now:** Ready for scope expansion or the next role within 3–6 months.
- **2 — Building:** On track for scope expansion or the next role in 6–12 months.
- **1 — Developing:** Not yet ready for scope expansion. Focused on mastering the current role.

**Values Alignment** is rated per value (Customer First, Step Into the Arena, Flock to Problems, Give Energy); the overall score is the rounded average of the four.

- **3 — Exceeding:** A role model. Actively reinforces the value in others.
- **2 — Meeting:** Consistently demonstrates the value day to day. Reliable and visible.
- **1 — Below:** Rarely demonstrates it without prompting. Inconsistent or absent.

**Engagement** measures energy, effort and connection to the team.

- **3 — Exceeding:** Deeply invested. Proactively contributes ideas, supports teammates, takes on challenges.
- **2 — Meeting:** Present and participative. Completes work with a reasonable attitude. Contributes when asked.
- **1 — Below:** Withdrawn, minimal discretionary effort, low energy. Limited voluntary contribution.

## The two 9-box placements

Your manager's four ratings place you on two grids. Each placement has a name and a prescribed action your manager commits to.

**Talent Density: Performance × Growth Readiness.** The capability lens: are you delivering, and how ready are you for more?

| | Perf Below (1) | Perf Meeting (2) | Perf Exceeding (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ready Now (3)** | Remove the Blockers | Stretch & Develop | Ready to Promote |
| **Building (2)** | Build the Foundations | Keep Growing | Expand the Role |
| **Developing (1)** | Intervene Now | Maintain & Recognise | Deepen the Expertise |

**Cultural Momentum: Values Alignment × Engagement.** The cultural and motivational lens: are you showing up the right way, and do you want to be here?

| | Values Below (1) | Values Meeting (2) | Values Exceeding (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engaged Exceeding (3)** | Redirect the Energy | Reinforce the Behaviours | Protect & Amplify |
| **Engaged Meeting (2)** | Have the Conversation | Stay the Course | Invest Deeper |
| **Engaged Below (1)** | Assess the Fit | Find What's Draining | Re-energise Now |

For Analysis, the nine boxes roll up into three tiers: **Top** (Ready to Promote, Stretch & Develop, Expand the Role), **Medium** (Keep Growing, Deepen the Expertise, Remove the Blockers) and **Low** (Intervene Now, Build the Foundations, Maintain & Recognise), with the equivalent mapping for Cultural Momentum.

## How a cycle runs, step by step

1. **Cycle opens.** Everyone gets an email. Your dashboard shows the cycle, its three dates and a *Start Self-Assessment* button.
2. **Record results.** On the first page of your self-assessment you record the actual result against each key metric for the quarter and mark it hit or missed. Your manager sees the same numbers.
3. **Self-assessment.** You complete the five sections and submit. Your manager is emailed.
4. **Manager assessment, blind.** Your manager rates each section *before* the app lets them see what you wrote for it. Then they reveal your answers and write a response. They submit.
5. **Ready to Meet.** Once both are in, you move to Ready to Meet on your manager's board.
6. **Share.** Your manager shares their review. You get an email; your ratings, their comments and responses appear on your dashboard and under My Reviews. Read it before the 1:1. If your manager changes anything after sharing they must give a reason, and you'll see "Updated since shared".
7. **The 1:1.** Run from the meeting page, which shows your placements, what you both said side by side, your goals and results, and the notes and agreed actions. Your manager marks the meeting complete; the notes appear on your My Reviews and agreed actions land on your Tasks page.
8. **Results sent.** Your manager sends results to close out. The assessment is locked (your manager can still add to their responses).
9. **Act.** You and your manager work the agreed actions and goals through the quarter. Goal progress can be updated by either of you at any time; each of you keeps your own update.

## What you'll see in My Reviews

- A performance summary: your latest ratings and your average across cycles.
- For each cycle: your Talent Density and Cultural Momentum placements with the prescribed actions; the full comparison of your answers and your manager's, section by section, including their responses; a note if the review was updated after it was shared; the notes from your 1:1 once it's complete; and a history of any edits to your own self-assessment.
- The manager's side only appears once they've shared or sent results. Until then you see your own answers and the status of theirs.
